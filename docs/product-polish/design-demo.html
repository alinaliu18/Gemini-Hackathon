    Put a number on the result.

    Sign-ups went up a lot after that, and people really liked it.

    Try: give a before and after, even a rough one. "Completion went from about 40% to 55% in two weeks."

    Slow down when you explain numbers.

    Try: stop for a beat after each number. It gives the interviewer time to write it down.
